About Sigaram

A women-owned staffing firm built by technologists.

We started Sigaram because hiring great engineers had become a keyword-matching exercise — and we knew there was a better way.

Our story

Hiring, the way we'd want to be hired

Sigaram LLC was founded in Maryland with a simple frustration: too many hiring processes filter people through portals and parsers, while real engineering judgment — the thing that actually predicts success — never gets evaluated.

So we built the firm we wished existed. Every candidate we present has been through technical conversation with people who've built production systems themselves. We send a shortlist of the right five, not a spreadsheet of fifty.

We're a women-owned startup, founder-led and deliberately small — which means every client gets senior attention, honest timelines, and a partner who's personally invested in every placement.

The name Sigaram (சிகரம்) means "peak" or "summit" in Tamil — the standard we hold every placement to.
