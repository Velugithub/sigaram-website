Three straightforward engagement models — one rigorous vetting process behind all of them. Every candidate is screened by working technologists before they ever reach your inbox.

Direct-Hire Placements

Your next full-time engineer, delivered as a shortlist

We source, technically vet, and deliver candidates you can interview this week — for the permanent roles that define your team.

  • Role calibration

    A working session to define must-haves, team fit, and compensation bands before we source a single resume.

  • Targeted sourcing

    We recruit across professional networks and communities — not just whoever applies to a job board.

  • Practitioner screening

    Technical conversations led by engineers, plus reference checks before you meet anyone.

  • Replacement guarantee

    If the hire doesn't work out within the guarantee period, we replace them at no additional charge.

Contract Staffing

Senior capacity, exactly when you need it

How it compares

Pick the model that matches the need

Direct-Hire

Best for: core team roles you'll grow around. One-time fee, due only on a successful start.

Contract

Best for: projects, backfills, and surge capacity. Simple hourly bill rate, flexible duration.

Contract-to-Hire

Best for: roles where on-the-job fit matters most. Evaluate for a contract period, then convert to full-time with no placement surprise.
